Subject: Request for Proposal — "My Bus" Transport Management System

Dear [Your Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing on behalf of our bus transport company to formally request the development of a web-based transport management system that we are calling "My Bus."

Our company operates scheduled city-to-city bus trips. At the core of our operation, we need a system that allows passengers to purchase tickets for trips. To begin with, all passengers must be registered account holders — we will not support ephemeral or walk-in passengers in the initial version. Registered passengers should have access to their trip history, applicable discounts, and notifications regarding their bookings. When a ticket is purchased, the passenger must receive a receipt that can be presented at check-in before boarding.

Each trip is assigned a single bus. The motorist (driver) is responsible for starting and ending each trip directly within the system. Administrators must be able to create and manage new trips.

An important requirement is full compliance with both LGPD (Brazilian data protection law) and GDPR (European data protection regulation), as passenger data privacy is a priority for us.

To keep the initial scope manageable, we have decided not to integrate any third-party platforms at this stage. We want the MVP to be simple, self-contained, and focused on the core functionality described above.

I look forward to discussing this project further and hearing your thoughts on how we can bring "My Bus" to life.

Best regards,

[Bus Company Manager Name]
[Company Name]
