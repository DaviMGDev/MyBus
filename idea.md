# My Bus is intended to be a transport management system 
web based 

a company offers trips city to city with buses 

to take a trip the passenger shall buy a ticket.

passengers that have account have access to history and discount and notifications and etc. // for the mvp, no ephemeral passengers, only registered.

a trip have only one bus 

the motorist is the one that starts the trip and ends it in the system 

the admin can make add new trips 

must be compliant with LGPD and GDPR 

a passenger can buy tickets for a trip 

the passenger will receive a receipt to show at the checkin(?)

I dont what more to include in the core of the system. I want to keep it simple. the mvp will not use any third party platform integration
